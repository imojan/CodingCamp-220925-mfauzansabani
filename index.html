Our Profile

About Velofrent

Velofrent is a motorcycle rental service. We provide well-maintained scooters and manual bikes for daily commuting, short trips, and weekend rides. Our focus is simple: clear pricing, quick handover, and reliable units—so you can move around the city with ease.

Vision

To be the most practical and trusted motorcycle rental partner for urban mobility.

Mission

  • Offer clean, safe, and regularly serviced motorbikes.
  • Provide transparent rates with flexible rental periods.
  • Deliver responsive support for booking, pickup, and returns.
Safety First

Periodic servicing & safety checks before each handover.

Simple & Clear

Straightforward booking and no hidden fees.

On-Time

Punctual pickup/return with friendly assistance.

Services

  • • Daily / weekly / monthly motorbike rental
  • • Helmet & raincoat included (per unit)
  • • Optional delivery & pickup (by area)
  • • Support: WhatsApp / Phone during rental period

Requirements: valid ID, motorcycle license, and refundable deposit.
